How to play

Batting: Press SPACE as the ball reaches the plate. Use ← / → to aim.

Pitching: When the sides switch, choose a pitch with 1, 2, or 3, aim with ← / →, then press SPACE.

Fielding: On a ball in play, use the arrow keys to move the fielder toward the ball. Press SPACE to throw.

Fan-made arcade baseball prototype. No MLB teams, players, logos, or licensed content. Best on a computer with a keyboard.
